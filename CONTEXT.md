# Field vocabulary

- **Ironstone**: finite blue ore deposits; smelted into plates.
- **Belt**: directional tile carrying one physical item.
- **Arm**: powered device transferring one item from its rear tile to its front tile.
- **Pole network**: poles joined within six tiles; supplies consumers within four tiles.
- **Survey pack**: research consumable assembled from one gear and one plate.
- **Relay beacon**: final transmitter, unlocked by eight researched survey packs.
