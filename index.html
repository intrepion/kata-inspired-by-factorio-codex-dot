◈ BRASSWAKETHE RELAY FIELD

EXPEDITION 07 / RECOVERY

Wake the quiet coast.

The old relay is silent. Turn this mineral field into a factory, then send a signal home.

WASD move · click build / inspect · R rotate · Q clear hand · F mine · right click recover · E inventory · wheel zoom · Space pause

A field engineer’s notebook

Build within 6 tiles. Walk with WASD. Select a machine below, point at a tile and click. R rotates the arrow; Q clears your hand. Click an existing machine to inspect it. Right click recovers its machine and stored items. Hold F over bare deposits to mine.

First working line

Place a drill on blue ironstone. Its arrow ejects ore into the adjacent belt. Belts feed belts, but machines need arms: an arm picks up behind itself and puts down ahead. Leave a tile for the arm between a belt and a furnace.

Place a generator and coal it from its panel. Place poles within 4 tiles of machines; poles link within 6 tiles. Each generator provides 24 kW. Furnaces burn their own coal and need no electricity.

Smelt ore into plates. An assembler makes gears from 2 plates, or survey packs from 1 gear + 1 plate. Change the recipe only when its inventories are empty. Feed 8 packs into a powered lab. Craft and power a beacon for 10 seconds to win.

Use the panel to insert materials and collect outputs. E opens your inventory and manual crafting. Manual crafting cannot smelt plates or make survey packs. Coal is the dark patch south; stone is southeast. Saves are local to this browser. A fresh expedition replaces only this game’s save.
